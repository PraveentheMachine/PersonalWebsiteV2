### Video which had me thinking

The London Diaries series by Daniel Simmons is one of my favourite YouTube series to follow. He’s got a dope aesthetic and a cool lifestyle mixed in with some great advice makes it an all timer. What got me was @15:11 where he talks about learning something new or starting over.

“You can either look at how bad you are and fixate on that and you will never feel good about yourself… or you can accept where you are… it's the starting point and focus on the micro level improvement. Day after day week after week… You can’t compare yourself because your circumstances are so unique… it's going to get better”

Really stuck out to me, I’m trying a bunch of new things (like this blog) and I could hyper focus on my workflow not being perfect, I am not the perfect writer or the design not being super aesthetic or I don’t have much content so it looks ‘weird,’ correct word would be unpolished. But rather I’m focusing on the metrics that are improving, I wrote a blog post and deployed the code. Just something to keep in mind with the whole comparison battle we have.

## Why did I create a Blog?

I think I’m a pretty regular guy but I’ve always wanted to make content or have some sort of creative expression. Creating a blog was a natural first step after enjoying journaling. I don’t plan to have a super consistent format, it will mostly be whatever I’m doing and learning whether that’s tech topics (concurrency, leetcode, database design, system design stuff) and writing it down and explaining it like I’m five to solidify the knowledge or just posting about whatever as I type this out on a Google Doc I feel very chilled.
