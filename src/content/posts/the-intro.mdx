## Intro

First blog post, welcome! This has been something I’ve been thinking about doing for a while but just never got around to. I had a free weekend, so I smashed this out. It's been fun to do, and I finally got around to refactoring my old website from uni. Inspired by content creators like Ali Abdaal and my bro, I’ve actually decided to blog stuff. What stuff? I guess we'll see, but expect a lot of different changes and design updates as I fiddle around with things. Is it really a project created by me if I don’t use Tailwind? I’ve built this with Astro as the framework of choice, PNPM for my package manager, and deployments are done with Vercel. You could call it a hypebeast stack.

## Why did I create a Blog?

I think I’m a pretty regular guy, but I’ve always wanted to create content or have some sort of creative expression. Creating a blog was a natural first step after enjoying journaling. I don’t plan to have a super consistent format; it will mostly be whatever I’m doing and learning. Whether that’s tech topics (concurrency, Leetcode, database design, system design stuff) and writing it down and explaining it like I’m five to solidify the knowledge, or just posting about whatever, as I type this out on a Google Doc, I feel very chilled.

## Life Stuff

I journal, so none of my spicy stuff should appear here, haha. If you stumble upon this blog, I cannot recommend journaling highly enough. I wish I did it more often. You gain so much more context on why you made decisions in the moment, and if you write down your goals, it’s the coolest feeling in the world when you look back and see the stuff you achieve. For example, one of my goals way back in 2021 was to have the confidence to wear an NBA singlet to the gym. At the time, I was weighing in at 97 kg, and comparing that time to now and seeing that entry today was crazy. My favorite NBA jersey has to be the Vice City Miami Heat jersey.

### Good

### Bad

Being a US citizen living permanently in New Zealand sounds like a dream, and it is in many ways, but the pain of filing taxes and how that hampers your ability to invest is significant. Having to worry about Passive Foreign Investment Companies (PFICs) and how that relates to KiwiSaver, FIF tax (tbf, this is a New Zealand-based issue), and accurately reporting gains, among many other things, makes it a PITA. With New Zealand being small relative to the globe, it’s challenging to get the support I imagine expats in other countries receive.
